A little about us

Built by students.
For every learner.

LFHS Academic Hub is a student-built learning companion from the Little Flower High School community in Panaikulam. It brings study resources, planning tools and future-focused exploration together in one accessible place.

Whether you’re part of LFHS or learning at another school, you’re welcome here. The goal is simple: make learning feel more organized, more approachable and more enjoyable.
